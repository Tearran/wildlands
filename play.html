How to Play — 2-Card Playable Discard Edition

  • No 4-Card Market — Just Deck + 2 Playable Discard Piles (A & B):
    • In Draw Phase, draw 2 cards from the Draw Deck and/or take the top ♠/♣/♥/★ card off Discard Pile A or B.
    • In Play Phase, you can play a card from your Hand / Jackalope — OR click Discard Pile A or B directly to play that discarded ♠/♣/♥/★ card straight onto your board!
    • Anti-loop rule: Played ♦ Disturbances go to the Spent Storms stack (reshuffled when the Deck runs out) so Diamonds can't be ping-ponged back and forth every turn.
  • Unlocked 3-Slot Ecosystem: Play any ♠, ♣, or ♥ into its slot — empty or right on top of an old card (discarding the old card onto Discard A/B).
  • Diamonds (♦) & True-Wild Jackalope (★): Play ♦ to discard an opponent's played card (or Refresh to Draw 2). Play your Jackalope as ANY card — any Ecosystem slot (even overwriting) or a Wild Disturbance!
